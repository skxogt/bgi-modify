FAQ

Quick answers

Still unsure? Send us a question. A short message is enough.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Tell us what is happening in your business in your own words and we will suggest a sensible starting point. You do not need to choose a service first.

Can I just get a new website?

Yes. Many projects start with a website, landing page or sales material on its own. A business assessment is only useful when the bigger problem is unclear.

How are price and timing decided?

They depend on the work involved, the information available and how reviews will happen. We agree the scope, price and schedule in a written proposal before any work starts.

Who owns the finished work?

Ownership and usage rights are set out in your project agreement, including any third-party licenses such as fonts, software or stock images.
