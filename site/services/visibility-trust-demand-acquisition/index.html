Visibility, Trust, Demand & Acquisition

Help the right customers find, trust and choose you

BGI Solution helps you improve how potential customers discover your business, understand its value and move toward a buying decision.

Start with a specific problem or project. We agree what to investigate, what to create or change, and how to review the work.

Email BGI Solution ↗Explore Services

Where do you need help?

A customer may find you, trust you, ask a question, and still stop short of buying. The gap matters.

Illustrative journey from discovery through confidence and interest to inquiry and decision.

  • Customers cannot find you online. Review search needs, website content and business listings.
  • People find you but still hesitate. Review the proof, reputation and information that support trust.
  • Your knowledge is not reaching customers. Create useful content that answers their questions.
  • Too few inquiries fit your business. Review whom you reach, what you say and how you assess fit.
  • Interested prospects stop responding. Identify unanswered questions and obstacles to a buying decision.
  • Acquisition costs are unclear or rising. Examine spending alongside customer fit and new customers won.
  • Your offer is difficult to understand. Clarify whom it helps, what it includes and why it matters.

Start with one service or combine related work. The services below address different needs; you do not need all seven or a fixed sequence.

Explore the services

Help customers find you online

Digital Visibility & Discoverability Growth

Make it easier for relevant customers to discover your business. We review what they search for, how your website answers those needs and whether your business listings provide useful, accurate information.

Possible outputs include:

  • Visibility improvement plan. Identifies gaps in website content and listings, recommended changes and priorities for implementation.
  • Measurement plan. Defines how to review relevant discovery, website visits and actions such as inquiries, using available data.

The scope specifies which changes will be implemented and reviewed. Search rankings, traffic and inquiry volumes cannot be guaranteed.

Give customers evidence they can trust

Trust, Proof & Reputation Growth System

Help customers assess whether your business can meet their needs. We review common concerns, the evidence available to answer them and how genuine customer feedback is requested and handled.

Possible outputs include:

  • Evidence inventory. Records available proof, its sources, required permissions and where it could answer customer questions.
  • Trust and proof improvement plan. Identifies missing explanations, claims that need support and ways to present valid evidence clearly.
  • Review and reputation workflow. Defines how genuine feedback is requested and handled, who responds and when issues need attention.

Proof must be accurate and used with permission where required. The scope defines who maintains it and whether ongoing reputation work is included.

Turn your expertise into useful content

Authority, Content & Intellectual Asset Growth

Turn your expertise into content that helps customers understand a problem, compare options or complete a task. We identify priority questions, gather your knowledge and supporting sources, and plan how to create, review and share the material.

Possible outputs include:

  • Editorial and distribution plan. Connects customer questions with topics, content formats, review responsibilities and publishing channels.
  • Useful content and reusable materials. Agreed articles, guides or reference resources that draw on your expertise and credit supporting sources.

The brief defines what will be produced, ownership and usage rights, who publishes it and how its usefulness will be reviewed.

Attract inquiries that fit your business

Qualified Lead Generation & Demand Creation System

Reach potential customers who may need what you offer and give them a clear way to express interest. We define the intended audience, suitable channels and what makes an inquiry a good fit, then plan how inquiries are captured, assessed and passed on for follow-up.

Possible outputs include:

  • Demand generation plan. Defines the audience, messages, channels, criteria for a relevant inquiry and measures of progress.
  • Inquiry and handover workflow. Shows what information is collected, how fit is assessed and who receives each inquiry for follow-up.

The brief defines whether the work includes setup, campaign delivery, tools, spending or ongoing management, along with follow-up responsibilities.

Help interested buyers make a decision

Sales Conversion & Customer Decision Enablement

Understand what happens between initial interest and a buying decision. We review recurring questions, objections, sales information and next steps to identify where prospects need clearer answers or an easier way to proceed.

Possible outputs include:

  • Buying journey review. Maps the steps toward a decision, recurring questions and points where prospects hesitate or stop.
  • Decision-support improvements. Recommends clearer sales information, next steps and follow-up, with specific changes to consider testing.

The brief identifies which materials or changes will be produced and whether testing is included. Any test has an agreed change, measure and review period.

Understand what it costs to win a customer

Customer Acquisition & CAC Optimization

Customer acquisition cost, or CAC, describes the cost of winning a new customer. We define the costs, customer counts and time periods included, then examine spending alongside customer fit and sales progress using the available records.

Possible outputs include:

  • Acquisition cost model. Connects the included costs with new-customer counts so you can compare relevant channels or activities, where the data allows.
  • Improvement priorities. Identifies possible waste, poor-fit inquiries or obstacles to purchase and explains which changes deserve testing.

Findings identify missing records, estimates and uncertainty about which activity contributed to a sale. Any expected savings or efficiency gains are assumptions to test.

Make your offer easier to understand and evaluate

Offer & Value Proposition Optimization

Clarify what customers are being asked to buy and why it matters to them. We review the intended customer, problem, benefits, supported differences, objections, packaging and commercial assumptions behind the offer.

Possible outputs include:

  • Value proposition and offer options. Explain why the offer matters to its intended customer and propose clearer ways to describe, structure or package it.
  • Validation plan. Identifies the assumptions, messages or offer choices to check, along with how the evidence will inform the next decision.

This service focuses on the offer and its value. Sales conversion work addresses the steps toward choosing it. The scope defines any production, launch or testing included.

Agree how to review progress

We agree how to review the completed work. Where business results will be tracked after use, we also define the starting information, measures, review period and responsibilities.

Measures may include relevant discovery, inquiries that meet agreed criteria, progress toward a purchase or acquisition costs. Any reporting explains data gaps and other factors that may affect the result.

Many inquiries are a poor fit

Prospects keep asking the same questions

What we agree before starting

We agree the problem, selected services, deliverables, responsibilities, price, expected schedule and review points. The proposal also defines required access and any production, implementation, tools, campaign spending or ongoing management included.

Questions about these services

Which service should we start with?

Describe what is happening and the decision or project you need help with. We can discuss the relevant service, the information available and whether the cause needs more investigation.

Do we need a business assessment first?

No. You can start with a defined need, such as an offer review or content project. If the underlying problem is unclear, an assessment can be discussed as a separate service.

How are visibility, lead generation and sales conversion different?

Visibility helps relevant people discover you. Lead generation helps attract and capture relevant interest, with agreed criteria for fit. Sales conversion helps interested prospects understand their options and move toward a buying decision.

Can you work with our existing website and team?

We can discuss improvements using your existing website, content, records and workflows. The brief defines required access, who approves changes and how responsibilities are shared with your team or other providers.

Does the work include implementation and ongoing management?

Your proposal states what will be reviewed, planned, produced or implemented. Publishing, tools, campaign spending, testing and ongoing management are included when explicitly agreed.

How will we know whether the work helped?

First, we review whether the agreed deliverables meet the brief. Where business outcomes will also be measured, we use the agreed starting information, measures and review period, and explain important limits in the data.

Tell us where customers get stuck

Tell us what you offer, whom you want to reach and where the difficulty lies. Share what you have tried and what you want to improve. We can discuss a focused starting point and what the work should deliver.
