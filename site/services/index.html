Understand what each service covers

Visibility focuses on how customers find you. Lead generation covers attracting and qualifying inquiries. Sales conversion examines what happens after interest is shown. Your project scope identifies which part needs work and how it will be reviewed.

Website production creates agreed pages and materials. Research, planning and ongoing management have their own scopes. Services can be combined when useful, with each part and its responsibilities explained in the proposal.

Questions about choosing a service

How does assessment differ from research and strategy?

Assessment investigates a business problem. Research fills a specific knowledge gap, such as customer needs or competitor activity. Strategy uses accepted findings to make choices and sequence the work. Your needs determine which services are relevant.

Can I start with a website or business materials?

Yes. Share your project goal, audience and existing content. We can discuss a defined production project directly. An assessment is useful when the problem itself needs investigation.

Can services be combined in one project?

Yes. The proposal explains each part, its deliverables and any dependencies. Additional work is discussed and agreed before it is added.

What information should I share first?

Start with your business, the question or project, what you have already tried and what you need to decide. We discuss which records or access are necessary before you share business information.

Does planning include building or ongoing management?

Those activities are included when specified in the scope. For a website project, confirm hosting, publication and ongoing support responsibilities. For an improvement service, agree the review schedule and authority to make changes.

How do we agree pricing and timing?

We define the required work, inputs, deliverables and review arrangements, then discuss the price and expected schedule. Those terms are agreed before work starts.
