FAQ

Questions about our services

How is an assessment different from research or strategy?

An assessment investigates a business problem. Research fills a specific knowledge gap, such as customer needs or competitor activity. Strategy uses the findings to make choices and plan the work.

Can I just get a new website?

Yes. Many projects start with a website, landing page or sales material on its own. A business assessment is only useful when the bigger problem is unclear.

Can services be combined in one project?

Yes. Your proposal explains each part, what you will receive and how the parts depend on each other. Additional work is always agreed before it is added.

How are price and timing decided?

They depend on the work involved, the information available and how reviews will happen. We agree the scope, price and schedule in a written proposal before any work starts.
