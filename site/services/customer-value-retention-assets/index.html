Customer Value, Retention & Asset Production

Support customers and build useful materials

BGI Solution helps you improve the steps after a purchase, plan useful customer follow-up and produce websites or business materials for a defined purpose.

Tell us what needs to work better or what you need to build. We agree the purpose, outputs and responsibilities before work starts.

Email BGI Solution ↗Explore Services

Where do you need help?

Some projects improve the experience after a sale. Others produce the website or materials your business needs.

Two complementary tracks: customer onboarding and relationships, or practical website and asset production.

  • New customers need clearer guidance. Review the steps and responsibilities between purchase and the first useful result.
  • Customer relationships need attention. Review follow-up, support needs, reasons customers may leave and suitable opportunities to help them further.
  • You need a website or business materials. Define what to produce, who will use it and what it should help them understand or do.

Start with one defined need

You can choose one service or combine related work. A website or sales-material project can start directly, without a separate onboarding or retention engagement.

Explore the services

Help new customers reach their first useful result

Customer Onboarding & Time-to-Value Optimization

Make the steps after purchase easier to understand and complete. We review how customers get started, look for delays or confusion and define the first useful result they should reach.

Possible outputs include:

  • Onboarding journey review. Maps the current steps, customer questions, delays and gaps between expectations and delivery.
  • Onboarding workflow and milestone plan. Defines the next steps, the person responsible for each one and the milestones used to check progress.
  • Customer guidance and supporting materials. Agreed welcome instructions, checklists or messages that explain what customers need to do and what happens next.

A time-to-value measure records how long it takes to reach that result from an agreed starting point. The brief defines any process changes, materials or system setup included.

Strengthen relationships after the initial purchase

Customer Retention, Expansion & Advocacy System

Help customers continue to receive value after the initial purchase. We review available records, customer needs, follow-up and support communication to identify where relationships may need attention.

Possible outputs include:

  • Retention and follow-up plan. Defines when contact is useful, what support or information is needed, who follows up and which signals need attention.
  • Expansion opportunity review. Identifies additional customer needs your business could serve, with evidence of fit and assumptions that need checking.
  • Advocacy workflow. Defines how to request genuine feedback, referrals or customer stories, including the permissions and review steps required before use.

The brief separates workflow design from operating ongoing follow-up. It also defines responsibilities and any measures used to review retention or expansion.

Build the website or materials your business needs

Website & Growth Asset Factory

Create a website, landing page, sales material or useful resource from an agreed brief. We connect the content, design and production work with a clear audience, purpose and next step.

Possible packages and handover outputs include:

  • Website Growth Package. Agreed website pages and supporting files, including the specified page structure, copy, layouts for different screen sizes and features.
  • Conversion Asset Package. A landing page or related material built around an offer and a next step, such as an inquiry. Any form or connection to another system is specified in the brief.
  • Sales Asset Package. A service brochure, presentation, proposal document or other agreed sales material that explains the offer using approved information and supported claims.
  • Authority Asset Package. A guide or other agreed resource produced from approved expertise and sources. Planning a wider content program is separate work.
  • Deployment-Ready Growth Assets. Final files and instructions prepared for the agreed platform or use, with a record of completed checks and any setup still required.

Your proposal confirms the selected packages, content responsibilities, pages or materials, features, file formats and revisions. Ownership, usage rights and third-party licenses are defined in the agreement.

Launch, hosting, domain purchases and ongoing support are included only when listed in the agreement. The brief identifies who is responsible for each included activity.

Review the work before publication or use

The brief defines the project checks. For websites, these may cover content, navigation, mobile layouts, links, forms and agreed accessibility or performance requirements. For other materials, checks may cover accuracy, readability, layout and file usability.

The handover records completed checks, open issues and who is responsible for anything still needed before use.

Agree how to review progress

We first check whether the completed work meets the brief. If business outcomes will also be tracked after use, we agree the starting records, measures, review period and responsibilities.

Onboarding measures might include completed milestones or time to the first useful result. Retention measures may include continued use, repeat business or suitable expansion. For websites and materials, we define the actions or outcomes to review after use.

The measures depend on your business and available information. Reviews identify gaps and other factors that may affect the results.

Illustrative project scopes

The examples on this page are possible project briefs, not claims about actual client work or achieved customer results.

What we agree before starting

We agree the need, deliverables, responsibilities, price, expected schedule and review points. The agreement also defines required access, content responsibilities, revisions, rights and any implementation or ongoing support included.

Share the purpose, intended audience and existing materials or processes. We discuss the records and access needed before you share customer information.

Questions about these services

Can we start with a website or sales-material project?

Yes. Tell us what you need, who it is for and how it will be used. A website or materials project can start directly. Any wider research or strategy is discussed as a separate scope.

Can you work with our existing website or customer processes?

We review what exists before agreeing on changes. The brief identifies what can be reused, whether the existing platform or tools are suitable and what access or technical checks are needed.

Will you manage ongoing customer follow-up?

Your proposal states whether the work includes planning, materials, implementation or ongoing activity. Managing customer communication and follow-up requires an explicit scope and agreed responsibilities.

Are hosting, launch and ongoing website support included?

Your proposal states whether these activities are included. Where they are, we agree the responsibilities, terms and any external costs before work begins.

Who owns the finished work?

The project agreement defines ownership and usage rights for the commissioned work. Third-party software, images, fonts or other materials may have their own license terms, which are identified in the agreement.
