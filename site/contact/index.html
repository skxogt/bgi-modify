FAQ

Before you get in touch

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Tell us what is happening in your business in your own words and we will suggest a sensible starting point. You do not need to choose a service first.

Is the first conversation free?

A first message does not create a paid project. Any consultation, assessment fee or other arrangement is agreed with you before work starts.

Can businesses outside the United States contact you?

Yes. The United States is a priority market and inquiries from other countries are welcome. Meeting times and working arrangements are agreed for each project.

Should I attach files or private information?

Not in your first message. If a project needs documents or access, we agree a suitable way to share them first.
