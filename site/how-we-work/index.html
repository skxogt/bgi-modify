Getting started

Start with the question, not a service name

Describe a problem, an opportunity or something you need built. A single, clearly defined project is a good starting point.

  • You want to understand a problem.Tell us what is happening, what you have tried and which decision you need to make.
  • You need a practical plan.Explain what you want to change, what you already know and which constraints matter.
  • You need a website or business material.Share its purpose, audience, existing materials and where it will be used.
  • You have a system to improve.Describe what is running now and what seems unclear.

The process

A clear path from first conversation to handover

The detail varies for an assessment, a website or ongoing improvement work, but the stages stay the same.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Share your business question or project idea, who it should help and what a useful result would look like.

  2. Review the relevant information

    We look at existing documents, website materials or records that affect the work, and note any gaps or assumptions to check.

  3. Agree the project scope

    Before work begins we confirm deliverables, your inputs, review points, responsibilities, schedule and price, in writing.

  4. Produce the agreed work

    Findings, a roadmap, website content, design files or other agreed assets, with decisions brought to the agreed review points.

  5. Review and check the outputs

    You review the work against the brief. We check content, files or features to the extent specified in the project.

  6. Hand over and decide what is next

    We hand over the agreed files or findings and any open items. Further work is only added if you decide you need it.

Before we start

What we agree up front

A clear agreement protects everyone’s time. Hosting, domains, advertising, integrations and maintenance are only included when listed.

Purpose and outputs

What we are solving, creating or reviewing, and which deliverables you will receive, in what format.

Inputs and decisions

Who supplies business facts, materials, access and approvals, and who makes final decisions.

Price, schedule and reviews

What the price includes, a realistic timetable for the scope, and when your feedback will be needed.

Rights and third parties

Ownership of the finished work, plus any separate licenses for software, fonts, stock media or tools.

Quality

Review, checks and handover

Research and strategy

We review the question, the evidence used, key assumptions and the limits of the findings.

Websites and digital materials

We check approved content, page structure, links, mobile layouts, forms and accessibility, and record what was tested.

Ongoing improvement

We agree the systems, measures, reporting schedule and who can approve changes before reviews begin.

FAQ

Questions about working with us

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Tell us what is happening in your business in your own words and we will suggest a sensible starting point. You do not need to choose a service first.

What do you need from our team?

Usually a clear project goal, someone who can make decisions and any existing material. Specific records or access are agreed before you share anything sensitive.

Can you work with our existing website or materials?

Yes. We can review what exists and agree a revision brief. Reuse depends on the platform, files and permissions.

Does a project include launch or ongoing support?

Only when it is written into the agreement. Production, launch, hosting and ongoing work are listed separately so responsibilities are clear.

Share your goal and what you already have. We will suggest a useful first project.
